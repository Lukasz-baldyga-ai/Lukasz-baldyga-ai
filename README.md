### Hi, I'm Łukasz 👋

I turn complexity into clarity - connecting technology, data, product thinking and human experience into products people can actually use and decisions people can actually trust.

I'm not an engineer by background - I came up through design, UX and product management. What makes me different is that I *build* the AI systems I use: custom agents, agent skills and multi-agent workflows that run my own product operations, not just prompts I type into a chat window.

My focus is building AI capability that scales beyond one task - workflows, agents and systems that make product work faster, clearer and easier for others to adopt too.

**AI Systems & Agents I Build**
- Custom agent skills (Claude Code) that automate real workflows end-to-end - sprint/status reporting, content strategy & optimization, data visualization, workflow and config automation
- Multi-agent orchestration - pipelines that fan work out across specialized agents (research, drafting, review, verification) and synthesize the results into one output
- An AI-wired knowledge management system - an Obsidian "second brain" connected to an agent that plans, drafts and reasons over years of my own notes and decisions, not a generic assistant
- Piloting AI adoption inside a product organization - proving workflows on myself first, then bringing them to my team and stakeholders

**Projects**
- **CarpAI** *(in development)* - an AI "fishing colleague" for carp anglers. A decision-support app built around a carp-behavior engine (water temperature, pressure, oxygen, light, habitat, season) paired with a Claude-powered conversational advisor - offline-first for poor-signal lakes, mobile-first, private by default. Currently building the core behavior engine and mobile/offline foundation.
- **[Electron-todo](https://github.com/Lukasz-baldyga-ai/Electron-todo)** - a lightweight desktop task-capture app: global-hotkey activation, local-first storage, cross-platform (Windows/macOS/Linux), MIT licensed, published and installable today.

**What I work on day to day**
- AI-assisted product management - discovery, backlog, and stakeholder communication run through agent workflows, not manual overhead
- Product leadership for machine insight & data quality products - I set the vision and requirements for automated data-quality detection and escalation, and work closely with the engineering team that builds it
- Communication systems - newsletters, release notes and stakeholder updates that make delivery visible
- Small experiments, shipped fast - new tools, formats and workflows, reviewed honestly, kept or killed
